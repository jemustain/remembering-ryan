# Remembering Ryan

### Ryan William Alf
*October 10, 1985 - April 28, 2022*

Ryan wasn't the warm and fuzzy type - he was rough around the edges, quiet, and didn't waste words. But underneath that tough exterior was a man who showed his love through what he did, not what he said. He was the kind of partner who would fix anything that broke, who was always thinking of ways to make life better, who was protective and loyal.

This website was created for Ryan's son, so he can know the real Ryan - not some perfect fairy tale version, but the hardworking man who wanted to give us the world, one repair and thoughtful gesture at a time.
