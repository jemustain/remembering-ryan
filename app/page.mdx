# Remembering Ryan

### Ryan William Alf
*October 10, 1985 - April 28, 2022*

Hey, little man. These stories are for you.

Your dad died before you were born — I was about halfway through being pregnant with you. He never got to hold you, but he wanted you so badly. He used to talk about all the things he was going to teach you.

Your dad wasn't the warm and fuzzy type. He was rough around the edges, quiet, and didn't waste words. But he showed his love through what he did — fixing things, showing up, working hard to give us a better life. He was the kind of man who'd drive two hours after work just to make you dinner.

I wrote these stories so you can know him. The real him — not some fairy tale version, but the guy who fixed everything, who was protective and loyal, who showed love by doing, not saying.
