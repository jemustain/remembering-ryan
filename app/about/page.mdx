## About Ryan

Ryan was **Ryan William Alf** (October 10, 1985 - April 28, 2022). He was a contractor — tall, blonde, good with his hands. He could fix just about anything. He drove a big green F350 diesel truck that you could hear coming from down the street.

He died before you were born. I was 22 weeks pregnant with you. He never got to meet you, but he wanted you more than anything.

## About This Site

These stories are written like a children's book — simple enough that we can read them together when you're little, but real enough that they'll still mean something when you're older. They're not fancy or made up. They're just the truth about who your dad was, told by someone who loved him.
