# About This Website

## Purpose

This website was created as a loving memorial to **Ryan William Alf** (October 10, 1985 - April 28, 2022) and as a special gift for his young son. These stories are told in a children's book style — simple enough for a young child to enjoy, focused on the real, everyday moments that showed who Ryan was, and designed to grow with his son over time.
