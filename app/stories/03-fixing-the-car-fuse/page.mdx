# Fixing the Car Fuse

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/03-fixing-the-car-fuse/hero.jpg" alt="" />

Ryan was over at Julie's place when he remembered that broken car plug. He'd been thinking about it since their first date.

"That plug in your car - it's probably just a blown fuse," Ryan said. "I can run to the hardware store and grab a new one."

"You don't have to do that," Julie said.

"It'll just take a few minutes," Ryan said. "I know which one you need."

Ryan took his dog Balto with him in his green F350 truck. They drove to the hardware store down the road.

At the store, Ryan went straight to the automotive section. He looked through the fuses until he found the right one - the exact size and amperage for Julie's car. He grabbed a couple extras too, just in case.

Balto waited in the truck while Ryan ran in. When Ryan came back out, Balto's tail was wagging.

Back at Julie's house, Ryan opened the fuse box in her car. He pulled out the blown fuse and held it up to show Julie.

"See? This little metal strip inside is broken. That's why your plug wasn't working."

He popped in the new fuse and closed the fuse box.

"Try your phone charger," Ryan said.

Julie plugged her phone into the car charger. The charging light came on.

"It works!" Julie said. "Thank you so much."

"No problem," Ryan said. "It was just a fuse."

That's how Ryan was. He'd see something that needed fixing, figure out what was wrong, and just take care of it. He didn't make it complicated or wait around. He'd just do it.
