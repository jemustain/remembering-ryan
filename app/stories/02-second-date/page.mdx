# Second Date

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/02-second-date/dogs-playing.jpg" alt="Dogs playing together" />


<StoryImage src="/images/stories/02-second-date/dogs-playing.jpg" alt="Dogs playing together" />

Once upon a time, Ryan and Julie decided to go on a second date. After their first date went so well, they wanted to see each other again. This time they planned to bring their dogs.

Ryan had a husky named Balto. Balto was big and energetic with thick fur and blue eyes. Julie had a dog named Ryu who loved to run and play. They thought it would be fun to see if their dogs got along as well as they did.

They met at a park near a lake. When Ryan pulled up in his green truck, Balto jumped out and immediately wanted to meet Ryu. The two dogs sniffed each other and then took off running together.

"I think they like each other," Julie said, laughing as the dogs chased each other in circles.

Ryan and Julie walked around the lake with the dogs running ahead. They took a trail that went through some woods. The dogs would disappear into the trees and then come running back, tongues hanging out, looking happy.

Ryan and Julie talked as they walked - about their families, where they grew up, what they wanted out of life. The conversation came easy, just like it had on their first date.

When they got back to Julie's house, the dogs were tired from all the running. Ryan remembered something from their first date.

"That plug in your car - I've been thinking about it. It's probably just a fuse. I could check it real quick if you want."

Julie said sure, so Ryan opened her car and looked at the fuse box. He found the blown fuse.

"Yeah, that's the problem. I'll pick up a new fuse and get that fixed for you."

Julie invited Ryan inside to meet her cats. She had two of them - one orange and one black and white. The dogs came in and sniffed around the cats. The cats weren't too sure about the dogs at first, but everyone settled down.

Ryan and Julie ended up sitting on her porch for a while, just talking while the sun went down. They were starting to spend more and more time together, and it felt natural.

---

*The best relationships are the ones where you just want to keep hanging out.*
