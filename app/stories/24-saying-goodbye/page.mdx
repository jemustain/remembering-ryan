# Saying Goodbye

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/24-saying-goodbye/hero.jpg" alt="" />

Years before Ryan met Julie, he'd had an electrical accident at work. He was on a construction site and got shocked badly enough that it damaged his heart. Ryan had told Julie about the accident, but she didn't fully understand how serious the heart damage was or how hard his heart had to work every day just to keep him going.

Ryan seemed fine. He worked long days doing physical labor - building, fixing, climbing. He never acted sick or complained about his heart. If you didn't know about the accident, you'd never guess anything was wrong.

Then one Sunday in late April, Ryan started feeling sick. At first it seemed like maybe a cold or the flu - he felt tired and achy. But by Tuesday, he was getting worse instead of better. By Wednesday, he was really sick. He could barely get out of bed.

Julie drove him to urgent care. The doctor there examined Ryan and said he needed to go to the hospital right away. But at the hospital, they tried to admit Ryan without letting Julie come with him. Ryan wasn't having it.

"I want Julie with me," Ryan told them.

The hospital staff said she couldn't come back with him because she wasn't family - they weren't married yet. Ryan got upset. He walked out.

"Let's go to a different hospital," Julie said. "One that will let me stay with you."

They drove to another hospital. The emergency room was packed. They waited in the waiting room for hours. Ryan hated needles and was anxious about all the tests they'd have to do. Julie sat with him, holding his hand.

Finally, they got called back. The doctors took blood samples and ran tests. They found that Ryan had pneumonia that had turned into sepsis - a serious blood infection. His heart, already damaged from the electrical accident years ago, was working overtime trying to fight the infection. It was too much strain.

The doctors admitted Ryan and started him on antibiotics and other medicines. Julie stayed by his bed. Ryan was scared but trying not to show it. That was just how he was.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/24-saying-goodbye/ryans-eye.jpg" alt="" />

Over the next few hours, Ryan got worse instead of better. His heart was giving out. The doctors did everything they could, but there was too much damage. Ryan's heart was just too damaged.

On April 28, 2022, Ryan died. He was 36 years old.

Julie was 22 weeks pregnant with their son. She was going to have to bring their baby into the world without Ryan there.

In the days after Ryan died, Julie sat alone and talked to her belly, to the baby growing inside her.

"Your dad was a good man," she said quietly. "He wasn't perfect, but he tried hard. He wanted to give us everything. He would have loved you so much."

Julie made a promise right then - she'd make sure their son knew who his father was. The real Ryan, not some made-up perfect version. The man who showed love by fixing things and working hard and being there, even when it was hard.
