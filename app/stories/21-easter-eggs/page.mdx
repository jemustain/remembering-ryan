# Easter Eggs

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/21-easter-eggs/hero.jpg" alt="Colorful painted Easter eggs on little paper stands" />

It was Easter weekend, and Julie was about four months pregnant. She wanted to do something to celebrate.

"We should dye Easter eggs," Julie said.

"Alright," Ryan said. "I'll pick up some kits."

Ryan stopped at WinCo on his way home from work and bought a couple of egg-dyeing kits. The kind with the little tablets that dissolve in water to make different colors.

At Julie's house that evening, they boiled a dozen eggs and let them cool. Then they set up bowls with the different dye colors - red, blue, yellow, green, purple, orange.

They dipped the eggs in the dye, held them there for a minute, then pulled them out to dry. Julie tried making patterns by dipping part of an egg in one color, then another part in a different color. Ryan just did solid colors - simpler that way.

"This one looks like a rainbow," Julie said, holding up an egg she'd dipped in multiple colors.


<StoryImage src="/images/stories/21-easter-eggs/egg-dyeing.jpg" alt="Easter egg dyeing setup with cups of dye and colorful eggs on the table" />
"That's pretty cool," Ryan said.

While they were dyeing eggs, Julie felt the baby move. It was still early, just little flutters, but she could feel it.

"The baby's moving," Julie said.

Ryan put his hand on her belly. He couldn't feel it from the outside yet, but he tried anyway.

"Maybe we should have another baby right after this one," Julie said. "I want a big family. Like three or four kids."

Ryan looked at her. "Let's take it one baby at a time. See how you feel after this one's here."

"I'm serious," Julie said. "I want our kids to be close in age."

"We'll see," Ryan said. "First things first."

They finished dyeing the eggs and arranged them in a basket. The eggs looked festive sitting on Julie's kitchen counter.

It was a simple Easter, just the two of them dyeing eggs together, but that was enough.
