# Telling Dad

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/15-telling-dad/family-call.jpg" alt="Family phone call" />

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/15-telling-dad/family-call.jpg" alt="Family video call" />



Once upon a time, Julie wanted to tell her dad and stepmom about the baby in person. Her dad lived in another state, so she and her sister Monica decided to fly out together for a weekend visit. Monica was pregnant too and wanted to share her news at the same time.

"Can you watch Ryu and the cats again?" Julie asked Ryan.

"Yeah, I got it," Ryan said.

Julie and Monica booked their flights. Ryan drove Julie to the airport Friday morning.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/15-telling-dad/happy-grandparents.jpg" alt="Happy grandparents" />

At their dad's house that evening, Julie and Monica sat down with their dad and stepmom.

"We have something to tell you," Julie said.

"I'm pregnant," Monica said.

"Me too," Julie added.

Their dad and stepmom were shocked for a moment, then thrilled. Two grandbabies on the way at the same time.

"When are you due?" their stepmom asked.

"Around the same time," Julie said. "Within a couple weeks of each other."

They spent the weekend talking about the babies and their plans. Julie told her dad about Ryan - how they'd met, how he'd moved in, how he was getting ready for the baby.

"He sounds like a good guy," her dad said.

"He is," Julie said.

Sunday evening, Julie flew back home. Ryan was waiting at the airport to pick her up.

"How'd it go?" Ryan asked.

"They're excited," Julie said. "They can't wait to meet you."

Ryan nodded. Meeting the parents was a big step, but it would happen eventually.

*Sharing good news with family makes it feel more real.*
