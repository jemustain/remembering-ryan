# Girlfriend at Work

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/08-girlfriend-at-work/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie casual couple selfie" />

Ryan had a packed schedule that day. Two big jobs lined up — a porch that needed fixing and a fence that needed building. Both customers were expecting him.

Ryan called his employees to see who could work. Only one person was available that day - Tom. Ryan needed at least two people to get both jobs done.

Ryan called Julie. "Hey, you busy today?"

"Not really, why?" Julie said.

"I'm short on workers. Want to come to work with me? I could use an extra hand."

Julie had never done construction work before, but she said sure.

Ryan picked up Julie and Tom in his green F350 truck. Tom usually sat in the front passenger seat, but today he climbed in the back so Julie could sit up front. Ryan had his tools and lumber loaded in the truck bed.

They drove to the first job - a house where the front porch was wobbly. Ryan and Tom did the measuring and the hammering, replacing the weak boards. Julie's job was to carry tools back and forth and talk to the customer, keeping them happy while Ryan worked.

The customer had a lot of questions. Julie answered what she could and got Ryan when she didn't know something.

At the second job, they were building a fence in someone's backyard. Ryan needed someone to hold the fence boards steady while he drilled them into place. That was Julie's job. She'd hold the board exactly where Ryan needed it, and he'd secure it.

"Little to the left," Ryan would say. "Perfect. Hold it right there."

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/08-girlfriend-at-work/restaurant.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie at a restaurant" />

They worked all day in the sun. Julie's arms got tired from holding boards, but she kept going. She handed Ryan tools when he needed them and made sure the customers were happy with the work.

By the end of the day, both jobs were done. Ryan drove Tom home first, then took Julie back to her house.

"Thanks for helping out today," Ryan said. "We wouldn't have gotten both jobs done without you."

"No problem," Julie said. "It was actually kind of fun."

Julie had helped Ryan get through a busy day when he needed it. That's what partners do.

*Sometimes helping just means showing up when someone needs an extra hand.*
