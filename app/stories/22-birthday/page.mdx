# Birthday

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/22-birthday/hero.jpg" alt="Balto on the couch with the cat" />

It was Julie's 29th birthday. She was about five months pregnant at this point.

Ryan wanted to do something special for her birthday. He knew Julie's favorite color was purple, so he stopped at a flower shop and picked out a big bouquet of purple flowers - a mix of different kinds, all in shades of purple and lavender.

When Ryan gave Julie the flowers that evening, she lit up.

"These are beautiful!" Julie said. "Thank you."

"Happy birthday," Ryan said. "You ready to go to dinner?"

They'd picked out a new Thai restaurant to try. Julie had been craving Thai food, and Ryan had found a place that had lots of vegan options.

At the restaurant, they sat at a table near the window. The place had string lights and smelled like lemongrass and spices. They ordered spring rolls to share, coconut soup, pad thai, and curry.

"This is so good," Julie said, trying the coconut soup.

They talked about the baby while they ate. They'd had their 20-week ultrasound recently and had the picture with them. They kept looking at it, trying to make out the baby's features.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/22-birthday/ryan-eating.jpg" alt="Ryan eating at the table" />

"I still can't believe we're having a baby," Julie said.

"Yeah," Ryan said. "It's getting real."

After dinner, they walked around outside the restaurant for a bit, playing Pokémon Go on their phones. It had become their thing - wherever they went, they'd catch Pokémon.

"I got a Squirtle," Julie said.

"Nice," Ryan said, checking his phone.

Back at home, Julie put the purple flowers in a vase on the kitchen table. The ultrasound picture was still on the fridge. It had been a good birthday - simple, but exactly what Julie wanted.
