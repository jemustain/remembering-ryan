# Hot Water Heater

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/14-hot-water-heater/water-heater.jpg" alt="Water heater" />


Once upon a time, Julie went down to the basement to do laundry and stepped in water. There was a puddle on the concrete floor.

She followed the water back to the source - the water heater was leaking.

"Ryan, come look at this," Julie called up the stairs.

Ryan came down and examined the water heater. Water was dripping from the bottom of the tank. He checked the age of the unit - it was old, way past its expected lifespan.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/14-hot-water-heater/water-heater.jpg" alt="Water heater installation" />

"The tank's rusted through," Ryan said. "You need a new one."

"How much is that going to cost?" Julie asked.

"I can install it myself. We just need to buy the unit," Ryan said. "I'll take care of it."

They went to the hardware store and bought a new water heater. It was heavy - Ryan loaded it into the bed of his truck with help from a store employee.

Back at Julie's house, Ryan got his tools together. Early the next morning, he started the job. First, he turned off the water and drained the old water heater. Then he disconnected the gas line and all the pipes. The old water heater was heavy, but Ryan maneuvered it out of the way.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/14-hot-water-heater/plumbing-tools.jpg" alt="Plumbing tools and equipment" />

He positioned the new water heater exactly where the old one had been. Then he connected all the pipes, making sure everything was tight and secure. He hooked up the gas line and checked for leaks with soapy water. No bubbles - it was sealed properly.

Ryan turned the water back on and lit the pilot light. The water heater fired up.

"Go try the hot water," Ryan told Julie.

Julie went upstairs and turned on the sink. After a minute, hot water came out.

"It works!" she called down.

Ryan cleaned up his tools and the old water heater parts. The job had taken him most of the morning, but it was done.

"Thanks for doing that," Julie said.

"No problem," Ryan said. "That's what I do."

Ryan had saved Julie hundreds of dollars in installation costs by doing it himself. And more importantly, he'd made sure it was done right.
