# Puppies

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/13-puppies/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan cuddling a puppy with Balto nearby" />

Ryan got a call from Tim, his old roommate. They'd lived together years ago and had stayed in touch.

"Hey man, I need a favor," Tim said. "My dog had puppies and I need to find homes for them. Can you help?"

Tim didn't have the time or space to keep five puppies around until they all found homes.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/13-puppies/ryan-with-dogs.jpg" alt="Ryan with Balto and the dogs" />

"Yeah, I'll take care of it," Ryan said.

Ryan drove to Tim's place and picked up all five puppies in his truck. They were about eight weeks old - old enough to leave their mom. He brought them back to Julie's house.

Julie wasn't expecting five puppies. "Are we keeping all of these?"

"Just until we find them homes," Ryan said.

Ryan and Julie made signs and posted them around town. They told people they knew who might want a dog. They screened families to make sure the puppies would go to good homes - places with yards, people who had time to train a dog.

Over the next couple weeks, four of the puppies found families. One by one, Ryan and Julie handed them over to their new owners.

But the fifth puppy was harder to place. He was energetic and mischievous - he chewed on everything he could get his mouth on. Ryan started calling him Chewy.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/13-puppies/puppies-sleeping.jpg" alt="The puppies sleeping together on the couch" />

Ryan kept Chewy for a few more weeks, even bringing him along in his truck to work sites sometimes. Chewy would chew on wood scraps and get into Ryan's toolbox.

"We might have to keep this one," Julie said.

"We'll find him a home," Ryan said. "He just needs the right family."

Finally, they found a family with a big yard and kids who wanted an active dog. The family had experience with high-energy dogs and thought Chewy would be perfect.

Ryan met the family and made sure they knew what they were getting into with Chewy. Then he handed him over.

All five puppies ended up in good homes. Ryan had told Tim he'd take care of it, and he did. That's what he did when someone needed help - he'd follow through until it was done.

---

*When you tell someone you'll help, you see it through to the end.*
