# The baby

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/09-the-baby/announcement.jpg" alt="Special announcement" />

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/09-the-baby/announcement.jpg" alt="Happy pregnancy news" />



Once upon a time, the day after Christmas, Julie took a pregnancy test. She'd been feeling off for a few days and decided to check.

The test was positive.

Julie called Ryan. "Can you come over?"

"What's up?" Ryan asked.

"Just come over. I need to tell you something."

When Ryan got to Julie's house, she showed him the pregnancy test.

"We're having a baby," Julie said.

Ryan looked at the test, then at Julie. "Really?"

"Yeah."

Ryan was quiet for a moment, processing it. They hadn't been trying to get pregnant, but they'd talked about wanting kids eventually. It was just happening sooner than they expected.

"Okay," Ryan said. "We're having a baby."

He wasn't the type to jump up and down or get all emotional about it, but Julie could tell he was excited in his own way.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/09-the-baby/baby-shoes.jpg" alt="Tiny baby shoes" />

They sat on Julie's couch by the wood stove and talked about what this meant. They'd need to figure out living arrangements - Ryan was still staying at his place most nights. They talked about names. They talked about what kind of life they wanted to give their kid.

"I want to teach them how to build things," Ryan said. "Useful stuff."

"We'll be good parents," Julie said.

"Yeah," Ryan agreed.

The next day, they went to the doctor to confirm the pregnancy and make sure everything was okay. The doctor said Julie was about six weeks along and the baby looked healthy.

When they called their families to share the news, they found out something funny - Ryan's sister Chrissy was also pregnant, and so was Julie's sister Monica. All three babies were due around the same time.

"Three cousins all born together," Ryan said. "That's pretty cool."

Ryan was going to be a dad. It was a big change, but he was ready for it.

*Life changes fast, and sometimes you just roll with it.*
