# The Rings

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/18-the-rings/hero.jpg" alt="" />

Ryan and Julie were planning to get married before the baby arrived. They needed wedding rings.

"We should go look at rings," Julie said.

"Alright, where do you want to go?" Ryan asked.

"The mall has some jewelry stores," Julie said. "And we can play Pokémon Go while we're there."

They drove to the mall in Ryan's truck. They walked around for a bit, catching Pokémon on their phones. Then they headed to one of the jewelry stores.

The salesperson showed them trays of wedding rings. Julie tried on different styles - some with small diamonds, some plain, some with designs etched into them. Ryan tried on a few basic bands. He didn't want anything flashy.

"What do you think of this one?" Julie asked, holding up her hand with a silver ring with small stones.

"Looks good," Ryan said.

Julie tried on a few more, then came back to the silver one. "I think this is the one."

Ryan picked out a simple tungsten band. It was plain, durable, and wouldn't scratch easily - practical for someone who worked with his hands.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/18-the-rings/couple-forest.jpg" alt="" />

They told the salesperson which rings they wanted. The rings would need to be sized, so they'd come back to pick them up in a couple weeks, before the wedding.

On the way out of the mall, they caught a few more Pokémon.

"That was easier than I thought it would be," Ryan said.

"You thought ring shopping would be hard?" Julie asked.

"I thought you'd want to try on like a hundred rings," Ryan said.

Julie laughed. "I'm not that picky."

They had their wedding rings picked out. One more thing checked off the list.
