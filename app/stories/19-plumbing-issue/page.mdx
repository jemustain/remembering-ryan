# Plumbing Issue

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/19-plumbing-issue/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan fixing plumbing under the sink" />

Ryan noticed a valve under Julie's bathroom sink was leaking. Water was dripping slowly but steadily, which would cause problems if left alone.

"I'll fix that valve this evening," Ryan said.

After work, Ryan got his tools and turned off the main water to the house so he could work safely. He crawled under the sink and started taking apart the old valve.

But when he got the valve apart, he realized he needed a replacement part - a specific washer that he didn't have in his toolbox.

"What time is it?" Ryan asked Julie.

Julie checked her phone. "Six-thirty."

"The hardware store closes at six," Ryan said.

They couldn't finish the repair without the part, which meant they couldn't turn the water back on. No water for the night.

"Well, that's not ideal," Julie said.

"We'll make it work," Ryan said. "I'll get the part first thing tomorrow morning."

They used bottled water to brush their teeth that night. They couldn't flush the toilet or wash dishes. It was inconvenient, but they dealt with it.

"This is like camping," Julie joked.

"Except without the fun parts," Ryan said.

The next morning, Ryan was at the hardware store right when it opened. He got the washer he needed and headed back to Julie's house. He finished installing the valve, tightened everything down, and turned the water back on.

He checked under the sink. No leaks.

"We've got water," Ryan called to Julie.

Julie turned on the bathroom faucet. Water came out normal.

"Thank God," she said. "I really need to shower."

Ryan had fixed the valve, even though it took longer than expected. Sometimes repairs don't go according to plan, but you deal with it and finish the job.
