# Christmas with the Family

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/07-christmas-with-the-family/hero.jpg" alt="Christmas tree with stockings" />

It was Christmas, and Ryan was bringing Julie to meet his family for the first time. Ryan's mom Billie was hosting Christmas dinner at her house like she did every year.

Julie was nervous. "What if your family doesn't like me?"

"They'll like you," Ryan said. "Don't worry about it."

Ryan picked up Julie that morning and they headed to Billie's house. They were running a little late — Ryan had forgotten his phone at home.

"Should we let her know we're running behind?" Julie asked.

"I don't have my phone," Ryan said. "Can you text her?"

Julie texted Billie from her phone to let her know they were on their way. She also mentioned that she was vegan, just in case that mattered for dinner.

When they arrived at Billie's house, she greeted them at the door with a big smile.

"You must be Julie!" Billie said. "Come in, come in!"

Billie had gotten Julie's text and had made sure there were plenty of vegan dishes Julie could eat, along with all the regular Christmas dinner food.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/07-christmas-with-the-family/cafe-selfie.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie out together" />

Ryan's family was all there — his sisters, his mom, nieces and nephews. It was a full house. Everyone sat down to Christmas dinner together. The table was packed with food and people were talking and laughing.

Julie sat next to Ryan and talked to his family. They asked her how she and Ryan met, what she did for work, the usual questions.

After dinner, they all gathered in the living room to open presents around Billie's Christmas tree. Julie got a homemade scarf and hat from Billie, in purple. Ryan and Julie had brought gifts for all of his nieces and nephews.

Even though they'd shown up late, it was a good Christmas. At the end of the night, Billie gave Julie a hug.

"You're welcome here anytime," Billie told her.

In the truck on the way home, Julie felt relieved. Ryan's family had welcomed her.
