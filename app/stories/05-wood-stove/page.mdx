# Wood Stove

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/05-wood-stove/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie in front of the wood stove" />

Julie had a wood stove sitting in the corner of her living room. It was one of those old-fashioned cast iron stoves the previous owners had left behind. She'd never used it — didn't really know how.

Winter was coming and Julie mentioned to Ryan that she'd never lit the wood stove.

"You've never used it?" Ryan asked. "That thing could heat your whole house."

"I don't know how to build a fire," Julie said.

"I'll show you," Ryan said. "It's easy once you know what you're doing."

The next time Ryan came over, he brought a load of firewood in the back of his truck. He carried it inside and stacked it neatly next to the stove so Julie would have it ready.

Ryan opened the door to the wood stove and showed Julie how it worked. "First, you need kindling - that's the small stuff that catches fire easily." He arranged some small sticks and pieces of newspaper in the bottom of the stove.

"Then you put slightly bigger pieces on top, and once that gets going, you add the real logs." Ryan lit a match and held it to the newspaper. The paper caught fire and the small sticks started burning.

Julie watched as the fire grew. Ryan added some medium-sized pieces of wood, then eventually put in a couple of bigger logs.

"You've got to leave the door open just a crack at first to let air in," Ryan explained. "Once it's really going, you can close it most of the way. And see this vent here? That controls how much air gets in, which controls how hot it burns."

They sat on Julie's couch near the wood stove as the room started to warm up. Julie's cats came over and curled up on the floor near the heat. The stove made the whole living room cozy.

"Now you know how to use it," Ryan said. "You'll save money on your heating bill."

Ryan had taken the time to teach Julie something useful. That was his way - if he could help someone learn to do something themselves, he would.
