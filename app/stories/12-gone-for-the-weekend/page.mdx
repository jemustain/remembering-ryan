# Gone for the Weekend

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/12-gone-for-the-weekend/hero.jpg" alt="" />

Julie needed to visit friends and family out of state for the weekend. She was only going to be gone for a few days, but she had Ryu and her cats at home who needed to be taken care of.

"Can you watch them while I'm gone?" Julie asked Ryan.

"Yeah, no problem," Ryan said. "I'll take care of them."

Friday morning, Ryan drove Julie to the airport. He helped her get her luggage out of his truck.

"I left their food and everything out on the counter," Julie said. "Ryu needs to be walked at least twice a day."

"I got it," Ryan said. "Don't worry about it."

While Julie was gone, Ryan went to her house every morning and evening. He fed Ryu and the cats, making sure they all had fresh water. He took Ryu on walks around the neighborhood - once in the morning before work and once when he got home in the evening. The cats were easier - they just needed food and someone to clean their litter box.

Ryan even spent one night at Julie's house instead of his own place, just so the animals wouldn't be alone all night. Ryu slept on the floor next to the bed, and the cats curled up on the couch.

Meanwhile, Julie was visiting with her friends and family. She told them all about Ryan and about the baby they were expecting. Everyone wanted to know about him.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/12-gone-for-the-weekend/couple-walking.jpg" alt="" />

On Sunday afternoon, Ryan picked Julie up from the airport.

"How were they?" Julie asked.

"Fine," Ryan said. "No problems. Ryu missed you though."

"Thanks for taking care of them," Julie said.

"That's what I'm here for," Ryan said.

Ryan had made sure everything at Julie's house was taken care of while she was away. He didn't complain about it or act like it was a big favor. He just did it.
