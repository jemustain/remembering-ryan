# Carpet Cleaning

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/16-carpet-cleaning/carpet-cleaner.jpg" alt="Carpet cleaning" />


Once upon a time, Julie's carpets were getting pretty dirty. With Ryu tracking in mud from outside and the two cats shedding everywhere, the carpets were looking rough.

Julie was down on her hands and knees trying to scrub out a stain when Ryan came in.

"That's not going to work," Ryan said. "You need a real carpet cleaner."

"I don't have one," Julie said.

"I'll go get one," Ryan said.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/16-carpet-cleaning/carpet-cleaner.jpg" alt="Carpet cleaning machine" />

Ryan drove to the store and rented a commercial carpet cleaning machine. It was one of those big units that sprays cleaning solution deep into the carpet and then sucks it back up along with all the dirt.

He brought it back to Julie's house and started cleaning the carpets. He moved furniture out of the way, filled the machine with hot water and cleaning solution, and went over every inch of carpet in the living room, hallway, and bedrooms.

The water that came out of the carpets was brown. The carpets had been dirtier than they looked.

"Gross," Julie said, watching the dirty water tank fill up.

"Yeah, but look how clean they're getting," Ryan said.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/16-carpet-cleaning/clean-carpet.jpg" alt="Clean fresh carpet" />

By the time Ryan was done, the carpets looked almost new. They were lighter in color and smelled fresh. He returned the rental machine to the store.

"Those carpets look so much better," Julie said.

"They'll get dirty again with the animals," Ryan said. "But now you know how to clean them. Just rent that machine every few months."

Ryan had cleaned the carpets but also shown Julie how to do it herself next time. That was more useful than just doing it for her.

---

*Some jobs need to be done over and over - that's just part of having a home.*
