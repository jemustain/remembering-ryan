# 20 Week Ultrasound

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/23-20-week-ultrasound/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie holding the ultrasound, both grinning" />

Ryan and Julie had their big 20-week ultrasound appointment coming up. This was the one — the anatomy scan where they'd get a detailed look at the baby and find out if everything was developing normally.

The appointment was at 3pm. Ryan was at a job site installing a fence when he checked his watch and realized it was already 2:30.

"I gotta go," Ryan told his crew. He packed up his tools quickly and jumped in his truck.

He called Julie. "I'm running late but I'm on my way."

"How late?" Julie asked.

"I'll be there. Just might be cutting it close."

Ryan drove straight to Julie's house and picked her up. They got to the doctor's office at 2:58 - just barely on time.

They checked in and waited in the lobby for a few minutes. Then a nurse called them back to the ultrasound room.

Julie lay down on the exam table and lifted her shirt. The ultrasound technician squeezed gel on Julie's belly - it was cold - and then started moving the wand around.

The baby appeared on the screen. Ryan and Julie both leaned forward to see.

"There's the head," the technician said, pointing at the screen. "And the spine. Let me get some measurements."

She moved the wand around, taking pictures and measurements of different parts of the baby. The heart, the brain, the limbs, all the organs.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/23-20-week-ultrasound/bench-kiss.jpg" alt="Ryan kissing Julie on a park bench while holding the ultrasound photo" />

"Everything looks perfect," the technician said. "Your baby is measuring right on track and all the development looks healthy."

"Can we get pictures?" Julie asked.

"Absolutely," the technician said. She printed out several ultrasound images showing different views of the baby.

Ryan and Julie looked at the pictures in the truck on the way home.

"This one you can see the face," Julie said.

"Kind of," Ryan said. "Hard to tell what we're looking at."

When they got home, they put the best ultrasound picture on the fridge with a magnet. Every time they walked by, they'd look at it.

Their baby was healthy and growing. That was all that mattered.
