# First Date

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/01-first-date/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie's first date selfie in the truck" />


Ryan was tall, blonde, and worked as a builder. He was good with his hands and knew how to fix just about anything that broke.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/01-first-date/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie's first date selfie in the truck" />

Ryan drove a big green F350 truck. It was a diesel that you could hear coming from down the street. He drove it to work every day, hauling tools and supplies for building projects.

One day, Ryan met Julie online. They started sending messages back and forth. Julie would tell long, funny stories in her messages, and Ryan thought they were hilarious. After talking for several months, they decided to meet in person. They picked Chipotle for their first date.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/01-first-date/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie's first date selfie in the truck" />

When Ryan walked into Chipotle, he saw Julie waiting for him. They got their food and sat down at a table by the window. They started talking and completely lost track of time. They talked about their jobs, their families, what they liked to do. Before they knew it, the Chipotle workers were saying they needed to close.

"Do you want to keep talking?" Ryan asked. "There's a lake nearby. We could walk around it."

Julie said yes, so they walked over to the lake.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/01-first-date/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie's first date selfie in the truck" />

They walked the whole way around the lake, still talking about everything. The sun was setting and the ducks were swimming around. By the time they got back to the parking lot, it was getting dark.

Ryan glanced at Julie's car and noticed something. "Your front tire looks low," he said. He walked over and checked it. "Yeah, you need air in this."

"I'll get it filled tomorrow," Julie said.

"I've got a compressor in my truck," Ryan said. He went to his F350 and pulled out his air compressor. He hooked it up and filled the low tire. Then he checked the other three tires and topped those off too, making sure they were all at the right pressure.

"Thanks so much," Julie said.

Ryan was putting the compressor away when he noticed something else. "Hey, there's a plug in your car that's not working right. I could fix that for you sometime if you want."

Julie smiled. "That would be great."

That's just how Ryan was. He'd notice something that needed fixing and he'd fix it. He didn't make a big production out of it or wait to be asked. He'd just do it.

After that first date, Ryan and Julie kept seeing each other. They'd go on more dates, and Ryan kept fixing little things around Julie's house. Eventually, they moved in together and became a family.
