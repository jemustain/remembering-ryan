# Picture Day

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/10-picture-day/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie selfie on the mall escalator" />

Julie was a few months along and wanted to take maternity photos to remember this time in their lives.

"We should take some photos," Julie said. "With the ultrasound picture and everything."

Ryan wasn't really into having his photo taken. He didn't see the point of posing for pictures. But he could tell it mattered to Julie.

"Alright," Ryan said. "Let's do it."

Julie found a couple of photographers who specialized in maternity photos. They picked a nice day and met the photographers at a local park.

The photographers had Ryan and Julie stand in different poses - holding hands, with Ryan's hand on Julie's pregnant belly, holding up the ultrasound picture. Ryan felt awkward the whole time, not really knowing what to do with his hands or where to look.

"Just look at each other," the photographer said. "Like you're the only two people here."

Ryan did his best. He wasn't comfortable in front of a camera, but he went along with it because Julie wanted these photos.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/10-picture-day/couple-selfie.jpg" alt="Ryan and Julie smiling together" />

After the photo session, they sat on a bench in the park and talked about their plans.

"I should probably just move in with you," Ryan said. "I'm at your place most nights anyway, and once the baby comes..."

"Yeah, that makes sense," Julie said.

That week, Ryan moved his stuff from his place into Julie's house. He brought his tools, his clothes, his furniture. Julie made room in her closets and garage for everything.

Once Ryan was moved in, he started working on the baby's room. He built a crib from scratch - measuring, cutting, sanding, assembling. He wanted it to be sturdy and safe. Julie helped him paint the baby's room a soft color.

They were building a home together and getting ready for their baby to arrive. There was a lot to do, but Ryan tackled it one project at a time.
