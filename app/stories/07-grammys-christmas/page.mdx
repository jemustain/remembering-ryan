# Grammys Christmas

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/07-grammys-christmas/christmas-tree.jpg" alt="Decorated Christmas tree" />


It was Christmas, and Ryan was bringing Julie to meet his family for the first time. His Grammy Billie was hosting Christmas dinner at her house like she did every year.

Julie was nervous. "What if your family doesn't like me?"

"They'll like you," Ryan said. "Don't worry about it."

Ryan picked up Julie that morning and they headed to Grammy Billie's house. They were running a little late - Ryan had forgotten his phone at home and had to go back for it.

"Should we let her know we're running behind?" Julie asked.

"I don't have my phone," Ryan said. "Can you text her?"

Julie texted Grammy Billie from her phone to let her know they were on their way. She also mentioned that she was vegan, just in case that mattered for dinner.

When they arrived at Grammy's house, she greeted them at the door with a big smile.

"You must be Julie!" Grammy said. "Come in, come in!"

Grammy had gotten Julie's text and had made vegan tacos especially for her, along with all the regular Christmas dinner food for everyone else. Julie was touched that she'd gone to the trouble.

Ryan's family was all there - cousins, aunts, uncles, and Grammy's friends. It was a full house. Everyone sat down to Christmas dinner together. The table was packed with food and people were talking and laughing.

Julie sat next to Ryan and talked to his cousins. They asked her how she and Ryan met, what she did for work, the usual questions.

After dinner, they all gathered in the living room to open presents around Grammy's Christmas tree. Ryan got a new leather tool belt for his construction work. Julie got a warm scarf. Julie had brought Grammy a potted plant, which Grammy loved and immediately found a spot for on her windowsill.

Even though they'd shown up late, it was a good Christmas. At the end of the night, Grammy gave Julie a hug.

"You're welcome here anytime," Grammy told her.

In the truck on the way home, Julie felt relieved. Ryan's family had welcomed her.
