# Dinner Surprise

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/04-dinner-surprise/hero.jpg" alt="Ryan in the kitchen with a dish towel" />

Ryan had a roofing job a couple hours away from where he lived. He was staying in a hotel near the job site for the week. One evening, he looked at a map and realized something — Julie's house was actually closer to his job than his hotel was.

Ryan called Julie. "Hey, I'm working near you this week. Can I come over and make dinner?"

"Sure," Julie said. "What are you thinking?"

"I'll figure something out," Ryan said.

Ryan knew that Julie was vegan, so he stopped at the grocery store on his way. He picked up tortillas, black beans, rice, vegetables, and ingredients for soup. He wasn't sure exactly what he was going to make, but he had some ideas.

When Ryan got to Julie's house, he was still in his work clothes - covered in dust and dirt from the roofing job. Julie told him he could shower if he wanted, so he did. Then he came out and started cooking.

Ryan chopped up vegetables and got the soup started on the stove. He cooked the black beans and rice, and started warming up the tortillas. Julie helped him put together the tacos.

"I didn't know you could cook," Julie said.

<StoryImage src="/images/stories/04-dinner-surprise/ryan-eating.jpg" alt="Ryan eating at the kitchen table" />

"I can make a few things," Ryan said.

They sat down at Julie's kitchen table and ate dinner together. The food turned out pretty good. They talked about Ryan's roofing job and how it was going. Julie told him about her week.

After dinner, Ryan helped clean up the kitchen. Then he had to head back to his hotel because he had to be up early for work the next morning.

"Thanks for letting me come over," Ryan said. "This was better than eating alone at the hotel."

Ryan had driven out of his way and taken the time to cook dinner, even though he was tired from work and had to be up early. That was just his way of showing he cared - by doing things, not by making a big deal about it.
