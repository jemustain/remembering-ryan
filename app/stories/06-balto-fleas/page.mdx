# Balto Fleas

<StoryMeta author="Julie" />

<HeroImage src="/images/stories/06-balto-fleas/dog-bath.jpg" alt="Giving a dog a bath" />


Ryan's dog Balto couldn't stop scratching. He'd sit down and scratch behind his ears, get up and scratch his side, then lie down and bite at his fur.

Ryan checked Balto's fur and saw little black specks moving around. Fleas.

"Balto's got fleas," Ryan told Julie over the phone. "I'm taking him to the vet."

"Oh no," Julie said. "Keep him away from Ryu and my cats until he's better."

"Yeah, I know," Ryan said.

Ryan loaded Balto into his truck and drove to the vet. The vet examined Balto and confirmed it was fleas. She gave Ryan some medicine - pills for Balto to take and a special shampoo for bathing him.

"Give him a bath every day for the next several days," the vet said. "And keep him away from other animals until the fleas are gone, or they'll spread."

Ryan took Balto home and immediately gave him a bath. He filled up the tub with warm water and lifted Balto in. Balto wasn't thrilled about bath time - he splashed around and got water all over Ryan and the bathroom floor. But Ryan scrubbed him thoroughly with the special shampoo, making sure to get all the fleas.

For the next week, Ryan gave Balto a bath every single day. It wasn't fun - Balto would try to jump out of the tub, water would go everywhere, and Ryan would end up soaked. But Ryan kept at it.

Julie kept Ryu and her cats at her house during that time. The dogs couldn't play together, which they both missed.

After about a week, Balto stopped scratching. Ryan checked his fur carefully - no more fleas.

"All clear," Ryan told Julie. "Balto can come over now."

When Ryan brought Balto to Julie's house, both dogs were excited to see each other again. They ran around the yard like they hadn't seen each other in months.

Ryan had taken care of the problem without complaining about it. That's what he did - when something needed to be dealt with, he dealt with it.
