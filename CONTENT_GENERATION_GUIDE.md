# Content Generation Guide
*Instructions for AI and human writers creating content about Ryan*

## Critical: Write Ryan Authentically

If you're using AI to generate content for this website, or if you're writing content yourself, you **must** understand who Ryan really was. Generic, warm, fluffy descriptions will completely miss the mark.

## Who Ryan Was

Ryan William Alf was **not** the warm-and-fuzzy, heart-of-gold, brought-joy-to-everyone type. That's AI-generated nonsense and it's disrespectful to his actual memory.

### Ryan's Real Personality
- **Quiet and rough around the edges** - He didn't waste words or show emotion easily
- **Not verbally affectionate** - He wasn't the type to say "I love you" constantly or express feelings openly
- **Protective partner** - He looked out for Julie and took care of her
- **Skilled and hardworking** - Excellent with his hands, could fix anything, was a builder by trade
- **Thoughtful in practical ways** - Showed love through actions, not words
- **Loyal and dedicated** - Once he committed, he was all in
- **Wanted to provide** - His goal was to give his family a better life, to give Julie the world

### How Ryan Showed Love
Ryan's love language was **acts of service**. He showed he cared by:
- Fixing things without being asked
- Noticing problems and solving them
- Working hard to provide
- Being protective and making sure Julie was taken care of
- Thinking of practical ways to help or make life easier
- Building and creating things with his hands

He did NOT show love by:
- Being verbally expressive
- Giving lots of hugs and kisses (though he wasn't completely unaffectionate)
- Saying flowery, romantic things
- Being cheerful and smiley all the time
- "Lighting up a room" or "bringing joy to everyone"

## Writing Guidelines for Content Generators

### When Describing Ryan's Character
✅ **USE:**
- "Quiet but caring"
- "Rough around the edges"
- "Showed love through actions, not words"
- "Protective partner"
- "Skilled builder and problem-solver"
- "Hardworking and dedicated"
- "Wanted to give his family the world"
- "Loyal and reliable"

❌ **NEVER USE:**
- "Warm and loving" (too soft)
- "Brought joy to everyone around him" (not true to character)
- "Had a heart of gold" (generic cliché)
- "Always had a smile on his face" (not his personality)
- "Lit up every room he entered" (completely wrong)
- "The most loving person ever" (not how he expressed love)
- Any generic, fluffy, AI-sounding descriptions

### When Writing Dialogue for Ryan
✅ **Ryan would say:**
- "I'll fix it." (short, direct)
- "Don't worry about it." (protective, practical)
- "I've got it." (taking charge)
- "Let me take a look at that." (problem-solving)
- "I can handle it." (confident, capable)

❌ **Ryan would NOT say:**
- "Oh sweetie, don't you worry your pretty little head!" (way too cutesy)
- "I just love helping you so much!" (too emotionally expressive)
- "This brings me such joy!" (not his way of talking)
- Long, flowery expressions of emotion (not his style)

### When Writing About His Relationship with Julie
✅ **DO:**
- Show him being protective
- Show him fixing things for her
- Show him being thoughtful in practical ways
- Show him wanting to provide and make life better
- Show him being a good, reliable partner

❌ **DON'T:**
- Make it sound like a fairy tale romance
- Have him being overly affectionate or mushy
- Use generic "soulmate" or "perfect love" language
- Make him sound like a romance novel character

### Tone for All Content
The tone should be:
- **Honest and authentic** - Don't sugarcoat who he was
- **Respectful and loving** - Honoring his memory while being real
- **Grounded in reality** - Based on actual personality traits
- **Warm but not saccharine** - Loving without being overly sweet

The tone should NOT be:
- Generic memorial language
- Overly sentimental or emotional
- AI-generated "perfect deceased person" descriptions
- Sanitized to the point of being unrecognizable

## Examples of Good vs. Bad Content

### Example 1: Describing Ryan

**❌ BAD (Generic AI Slop):**
> "Ryan was a beacon of light in everyone's life, a man with a heart of gold who brought joy and warmth wherever he went. His infectious smile and loving nature made everyone around him feel special. He was the kind of person who would give you the shirt off his back and always had an encouraging word to share."

**✅ GOOD (Authentic):**
> "Ryan wasn't the type to waste words or show a lot of emotion. He was quiet, rough around the edges, and you always knew where you stood with him. But he was the kind of partner who would notice something was broken and just fix it - no big deal, that's just what he did. He showed he cared by being there, by solving problems, by working hard to give his family a better life."

### Example 2: A Story Scene

**❌ BAD (Too Warm and Fuzzy):**
> "Ryan gave Julie a big, warm hug and smiled his beautiful smile. 'Oh honey, I love you so much!' he said enthusiastically. 'It makes me so happy to help you! This is the best day ever!' He practically skipped over to his truck, whistling a happy tune."

**✅ GOOD (Authentic):**
> "Ryan looked at Julie's flat tire and shook his head. 'I'll get it fixed,' he said. He didn't make a big deal out of it - this was just what he did. He grabbed his tools from his truck and got to work. That was Ryan's way of showing he cared: not with a lot of words, but by making sure things were taken care of."

### Example 3: About Page Content

**❌ BAD (Generic Memorial Language):**
> "Ryan was a beloved friend, family member, and partner whose light shone brightly in the lives of all who knew him. His gentle spirit and kind heart touched everyone he met. His memory will forever be a blessing to those fortunate enough to have known him."

**✅ GOOD (Real and Personal):**
> "Ryan wasn't the warm and fuzzy type - he was rough around the edges, quiet, and didn't waste words. But underneath that tough exterior was a man who showed his love through actions, not words. He was the kind of partner who would fix anything that broke, who was always thinking of ways to make life better, who was protective and loyal."

## For AI Tools Generating Content

If you're using ChatGPT, Claude, or any other AI to generate content for this site:

1. **Include this guide in your prompt** - Copy the relevant sections
2. **Be explicit about avoiding generic language** - Tell the AI "Don't use warm, fuzzy, generic memorial language"
3. **Provide specific examples** - Show the AI what Ryan would and wouldn't say or do
4. **Focus on actions over words** - Emphasize that Ryan showed love through what he did
5. **Review and edit** - AI will naturally gravitate toward generic "nice" descriptions. Edit them out.

### Sample AI Prompt

When asking an AI to write content, use a prompt like this:

> "Write a story about Ryan for his young son. IMPORTANT: Ryan was quiet, rough around the edges, and not verbally affectionate. He showed love through actions - fixing things, being protective, working hard. Don't make him sound warm and fuzzy. He was a good partner who wanted to give his family the world, but he showed it by doing, not by talking about feelings. Use simple language for a 3-year-old, but keep Ryan's authentic personality."

## The Bottom Line

**Write about the real Ryan, not some generic idealized version.**

His son deserves to know who his father actually was: a quiet, hardworking man who cared deeply but showed it through his actions. That's a real person worth remembering, not some AI-generated saint who never existed.

A man who shows up, fixes what's broken, and works hard to give his family the world - even if he doesn't say "I love you" every day - that's worth honoring. That was Ryan.
