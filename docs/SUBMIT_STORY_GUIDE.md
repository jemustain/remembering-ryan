## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Before you can submit a story, you need to:

1. **Be Signed In**: Click the "Sign In" button in the header and authenticate with your Google account
2. **Have Permission**: Only FAMILY and ADMIN users can submit stories
3. **Have a Story to Share**: Think about a memory you'd like to preserve

### Accessing the Submission Form

1. Sign in to the website
2. Navigate to the "Submit Story" page from the main navigation
3. You'll see the story submission form

## Writing Your Story

### Story Title

Your title should be:
- **Clear and descriptive** (e.g., "The Time Ryan Fixed the Truck")
- **3-100 characters long**
- **Free of emojis** (we keep it simple for young readers)
- **Unique** - each story should have its own distinct title

**Tips:**
- Start with "The Time..." or "When Ryan..."
- Focus on the main event or activity
- Keep it suitable for young children

### Story Content

Your story content should:
- **Mention "Ryan" at least once** - this is about him!
- **Be 10-5000 words** - can be short and sweet or detailed
- **Contain no emojis** - we use words to express feelings
- **Be child-friendly** - appropriate for young readers

**Writing Tips:**
- Write in a warm, conversational tone
- Use simple, age-appropriate language
- Focus on positive memories and moments
- Include sensory details (what things looked, sounded, felt like)
- Show Ryan's personality and character
- End with a positive note or lesson

**Markdown Formatting:**

You can use markdown to format your story:

```markdown
**Bold text** for emphasis
*Italic text* for thoughts or emphasis
### Subheadings for sections
- Bullet points for lists
```

### Content Guidelines (The Constitution)

All stories must follow our content constitution:

✅ **DO:**
- Keep content appropriate for young children
- Use the name "Ryan" (not "Daddy" or other terms)
- Write in a storytelling style
- Include positive character traits
- Use proper grammar and spelling

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use emojis anywhere in title or content
- Include inappropriate language or themes
- Make the story about yourself (keep focus on Ryan)
- Include overly sad or traumatic content
- Use abbreviations or text speak

## Adding Images

### Image Requirements

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF
- **Size**: Maximum 10MB per image
- **Quantity**: Up to 10 images per story
- **Quality**: Clear, well-lit photos work best

### Selecting Images

1. Click the "Choose Files" button in the Images section
2. Select up to 10 images from your computer
3. Images will appear as thumbnails below
4. Click the "×" button to remove any image

### Image Tips

- **Relevance**: Choose images that relate to your story
- **Quality**: Higher resolution is better (we'll optimize them)
- **Privacy**: Ensure you have permission to share the photos
- **Variety**: Mix close-ups and wider shots when possible
- **Orientation**: Both landscape and portrait work fine

### Image Optimization

Don't worry about file size - we automatically:
- Resize large images to appropriate dimensions
- Convert to WebP format for faster loading
- Compress without losing visible quality
- Generate thumbnails for the gallery

## Submission Process

### Before Submitting

1. **Review your story** for typos and clarity
2. **Check the warnings** - yellow text shows potential issues
3. **Verify image count** - you should see thumbnails of all images
4. **Read the guidelines** at the bottom of the form

### Real-Time Validation

As you type, the form will show:

- **Character count** for the title (0/100)
- **Word count** for the content (min 10, max 5000)
- **Emoji warnings** if any are detected (⚠️ remove them!)
- **Ryan mention status** (✓ "Ryan" mentioned)
- **Yellow borders** on fields that need attention

### Submitting

1. Fill in all required fields (marked with *)
2. Add optional images if you have them
3. Review the real-time validation feedback
4. Click "Submit Story"
5. Wait for the submission to process (usually 10-30 seconds)

### What Happens Next

When you click Submit:

1. ✅ Form validates all content
2. 📝 Images are optimized and processed
3. 🌿 A new branch is created in GitHub
4. 📄 Your story is formatted as an MDX file
5. 🖼️ Images are uploaded to the repository
6. 🔀 A Pull Request (PR) is created for review
7. 📧 You receive a confirmation with the PR link

## After Submission

### Confirmation Screen

If successful, you'll see:
- ✅ Success message
- 🔗 Link to your GitHub Pull Request
- 📊 Your PR number
- ℹ️ Next steps information

### Tracking Your Submission

1. **View Your Submissions**: Go to "Submit Story" → "View History"
2. **Check Status**: See if your story is Pending, Published, or Rejected
3. **PR Link**: Click to see the GitHub Pull Request
4. **Review Comments**: Administrators may leave feedback on the PR

### Submission Statuses

- **🟡 Pending**: Submitted and awaiting review
- **🟢 Published**: Approved and live on the website!
- **🔴 Rejected**: Not approved (check PR comments for feedback)

### What Reviewers Look For

Your story will be reviewed for:
- ✅ Constitution compliance (guidelines followed)
- ✅ Appropriate content for children
- ✅ Grammar and spelling
- ✅ Proper formatting and structure
- ✅ Image quality and relevance
- ✅ MDX syntax correctness

### If Your Story Needs Changes

If a reviewer requests changes:

1. Check the PR comments on GitHub
2. See what needs to be updated
3. The reviewer may edit directly or request a resubmission
4. You'll be notified when changes are made

### When Your Story is Published

Once approved:
- ✅ The PR is merged
- 🌐 Your story appears on the live website
- 📊 Status updates to "Published" in your history
- 🔗 You can share the direct link with family

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### "Title must be at least 3 characters"
**Solution**: Make your title longer and more descriptive

#### "Story must mention 'Ryan' at least once"
**Solution**: Add Ryan's name somewhere in your story content

#### "Emojis detected"
**Solution**: Remove all emojis (😀 ❤️ 👍 etc.) from title and content

#### "Image too large"
**Solution**: Ensure each image is under 10MB. Use image compression tools if needed.

#### "Too many images"
**Solution**: Reduce to 10 or fewer images

#### "Authentication required"
**Solution**: Sign in with your Google account

#### "Insufficient permissions"
**Solution**: Contact an administrator to grant you FAMILY role access

### Form Not Submitting

If the form won't submit:

1. **Check validation warnings** - yellow text shows issues
2. **Review error messages** - red text shows required fixes
3. **Ensure all required fields** are filled (marked with *)
4. **Try refreshing the page** and re-entering (form may have timed out)
5. **Check your internet connection**
6. **Clear browser cache** if issues persist

### Lost Your Work?

The form does NOT currently save drafts automatically. To avoid losing work:

- ✍️ Write your story in a text editor first (Google Docs, Word, etc.)
- 📋 Copy and paste into the form when ready
- 🔄 Don't refresh the page while writing
- ⏰ Submit before your session times out

### Getting Help

If you need assistance:

1. **Check this guide** for answers
2. **Contact an administrator** via email or messaging
3. **Review the PR template** on GitHub for detailed guidelines
4. **Read the Story Style Guide** for writing tips

## Best Practices

### Writing Great Stories

**DO:**
- 📖 Tell a complete story with beginning, middle, and end
- 💭 Include dialogue when you remember it
- 🎨 Use descriptive language (colors, sounds, feelings)
- 😊 Focus on positive character traits
- 👶 Write for a young audience (simple words)
- ✨ Share specific details that make the memory unique

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Rush the writing - take your time
- ❌ Include multiple unrelated events in one story
- ❌ Use inside jokes only adults understand
- ❌ Focus on sad or traumatic moments
- ❌ Write more than 5000 words (keep it digestible)

### Choosing Good Images

**Best Images:**
- ✅ Clear and in focus
- ✅ Good lighting
- ✅ Show Ryan's face when possible
- ✅ Capture the moment or activity
- ✅ Include other people in the memory
- ✅ Show emotions and interactions

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Blurry or dark photos
- ❌ Photos with sensitive information (documents, addresses, etc.)
- ❌ Images you don't have permission to share
- ❌ Duplicate or nearly identical photos

### Timing Your Submission

- ⏰ Allow **15-30 minutes** to write and submit
- 🕐 Don't start if you only have a few minutes
- 💾 Write in a separate document first if interrupted often
- ⏳ Reviews typically take **1-3 days** (be patient!)

### After Publishing

Once your story is live:

- 📢 Share the link with family members
- 💬 Encourage others to submit their memories too
- 🔄 Check back periodically for new stories
- ✨ Consider submitting more memories when inspiration strikes

## FAQ

### How long does review take?
Typically 1-3 days, depending on administrator availability.

### Can I edit after submitting?
Not directly. Contact an administrator or comment on the PR for changes.

### How many stories can I submit?
As many as you'd like! There's no limit (though we do rate-limit to 5 per hour).

### Can I submit stories on behalf of someone else?
Yes, but use your account and credit the original storyteller in the content.

### What if I don't have images?
That's fine! Images are optional. Text-only stories are welcome.

### Can I see others' submissions?
Only administrators can see all submissions. You can only see your own.

### Will my story definitely be published?
Stories must meet our content guidelines and pass review, but we aim to publish all appropriate submissions.

### Can I submit in a language other than English?
Currently, the site is English-only, but contact us if you have multilingual memories to share.

### How do I delete a submission?
Contact an administrator. You can't delete it yourself once submitted.

### Who can see my submission history?
Only you (and administrators) can see your submission history and status.
